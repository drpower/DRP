VOLUNTARY SAFETY RECALL

DR Power Pilot and Premier Trimmer Mower

The reason for the recall is to address a risk that grass and debris can accumulate in the product and ignite, posing a fire and burn risk.

This recall involves the following DR Power brand Pilot and Premier Trimmer Mowers:

Model Description Serial Range
T4X1076DMN DR TRIMMER PILOT 7.6 FPT DR MS All
T4X1076DMNR DR TRIMMER PILOT 7.6 FPT DR MS RECON All
T4X1076DMNSD DR TRIMMER PILOT 7.6 FPT DR MS SD All
T4X1176DMN DR TRIMMER PILOT 7.6 FPT DR MS All
T4X1176DMN-1 DR TRIMMER PILOT 7.6 FPT DR MS All
T4X1176DMNR DR TRIMMER PILOT 7.6 FPT DR MS RECON All
TR43076DMN DR TRIMMER PREMIER 7.6 FPT DR MS All
TR43076DMNR DR TRIMMER PREMIER 7.6 FPT DR MS RECON All


The model and serial number are printed on a white label that is on the side frame of your string trimmer. You can find the manufacture date of your unit on the serial number label affixed to the side frame of the unit, which is listed in YYYYMMDD format.

What will DR Power do?

DR Power is offering consumers a free consumer remedy kit. The remedy kit will include instructions for easy installation.

What we need you to do?

Please check your trimmer mower to verify that it is a recalled unit, based on the model numbers included above. If it is a recalled unit, please stop using the trimmer mower and fill out the form below for a free repair kit.

Expectation:

Please allow DR Power 1-2 weeks to review your request for a remedy kit. If additional information is needed, we will reach out with the email provided. Once we place an order for the remedy kit you will receive an email confirmation.

If you have any questions, please call us at 800-929-5018 from 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday through Friday or contact us by email at recall@drpower.com. Information about the recall can also be found at DRPower.com. Click on the “Product Recalls” link located at the bottom of the home page.
