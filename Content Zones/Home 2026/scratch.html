Extra Wide Means Extra Fast!

Step up to our widest and most powerful snow blower—the DR PRO XL-30B—built to take on big jobs. This model features a powerful 12 HP OHV engine that is designed with cold-weather operation in mind. Plug-in electric starting puts an end to hard starts—just run an extension cord, plug in, and you get sure-fire starts without the expense and battery maintenance that comes with a normal electric-starting engine. EZ-Turn™ steering makes for hassle-free 180º turns.

And our integrated control panel puts everything within easy reach: gear shift, auger and drive hand levers, chute rotation and deflection, heated hand warmers, and an LED headlamp. This machine has it all and is ready for the toughest winter conditions.
